Research Experience
======
* Ph.D. Student, Brown University, Current
  * **Redondovirus Phylogenetic Analysis**: Constructed a phylogenetic tree integrating publicly available Redondovirus sequences with novel samples generated from a Senegal-based cohort study of acute febrile illness, contributing new genomic context for this recently characterized respiratory DNA virus
  * **Machine Learning for Viral Genomics**: Completed a dedicated skill rotation surveying ML/DL-based phylogenetic inference tools and independently developed random forest and convolutional neural network (CNN) models to predict Lassa virus lineage origin from genetic sequence data
  * Supervisor: [Dr. Katie Siddle](https://vivo.brown.edu/display/ksiddle)

* Research Assistant II, Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health, January 2025-June 2025
  * **Manuscript Coordination**: Organized a collaborative manuscript on the GnarlyGenotyper joint calling pipeline, supporting organization, writing, and communication across contributors to document improvements in *Plasmodium falciparum* variant calling
  * **Epidemiological Inference from SNP Data**: Built a pipeline to analyze raw 24-SNP barcode data from *P. falciparum* samples across Senegal, estimating disease incidence using a trained Poisson model to infer key genetic metrics such as polygenomic fraction, monogenomic clone fraction, average COI, RH, and co-transmission rates
  * **Strain Frequency Estimation**: Applied linear algebra methods to infer the relative abundance of *P. falciparum* strains from MadHatter sequencing data, tracking their competitive dynamics in culture over time
  * Supervisor: Dr. Wesley Wong of the [Dr. Dyann Wirth Lab](https://hsph.harvard.edu/research/wirth-lab/)

* Master's Thesis, Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health, January 2024-December 2024
  * **Project Title**: Assessing the Performance of Whole Genome Sequence Variant and Joint Callers for Polygenomic Malaria (*Plasmodium falciparum*) Infections
  * Evaluated accuracy of two GATK pipelines (gGVCF and GnarlyGenotyper) across different *P. falciparum* sample populations from Senegal
  * Analyzed multiple datasets of 1,000 monogenomic and 3,000 mixed-genomic samples using Python
  * Assessed the impact of joint-calling accuracy by quantifying allele concordance and discordance across various polygenomic sample compositions
  * Discovered increased site accuracy with joint-calling on larger populations and highlighted challenges in accurate heterozygous site calling within polygenomic infections
  * Supervisor: Dr. Wesley Wong of the [Dr. Dyann Wirth Lab](https://hsph.harvard.edu/research/wirth-lab/)

* Undergraduate Honor's Thesis, St. Lawrence University, May 2021-May 2022
  * **Project Title**: The Ethology and Phenology of *Lasiopogon currani* (Diptera: Asilidae) in St. Lawrence County, New York
  * Documented behavioral patterns, including predation, test flights, positional rotation, mating, preening, avoidance, and aggression, by analyzing over 30 hours of field video
  * Identified 50 confirmed prey items captured by hand to estimate the diet of *L. currani* and analyzed over 600 potential prey specimens collected from malaise and pan traps, revealing a positive correlation between *L. currani* adults and prey abundance.
  * Identified that reduced precipitation and increased temperatures shortened the active season of *L. currani*
  * Estimated annual prey consumption of approximately 9,200 insects by the local *L. currani* population, highlighting their role in controlling populations of mosquitoes and midges, known disease vectors and human pests
  * Supervisor: Dr. Karl McKnight (retired)
